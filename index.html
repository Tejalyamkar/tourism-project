Top Tourist Attractions

Mahalaxmi Temple

The Mahalaxmi Temple is one of the most revered temples in Maharashtra, dedicated to the goddess Mahalaxmi. It is an important pilgrimage site and a historical landmark of Kolhapur.

Panhala Fort

Located in the heart of Kolhapur, the Panhala Fort is a symbol of the Maratha dynasty's strength and resilience. It offers a great view of the city and is a must-visit for history enthusiasts.

Rankala Lake

Rankala Lake is a picturesque spot for boating and enjoying the natural beauty of Kolhapur. It is surrounded by temples, making it a peaceful place for visitors to relax.

New Palace

Around the 1930s, the Shalini Palace was constructed. Princess Shrimant Shalini Raje, the daughter of Kolhapur's rulers, Chhatrapati Shahaji II and Queen Pramila Raje, was given the title. The Palace required a total of four years to complete as a magnificent landmark.

Jyotiba Temple

The Jyotiba Temple, dedicated to the deity Jyotiba, is a significant Hindu pilgrimage site located near Kolhapur, Maharashtra, with a rich history rooted in mythology and religious tradition, including its reconstruction by Maharaja Ranoji Shinde of Gwalior in 1730.

Rautwadi Waterfall

Rautwadi Waterfall is a picturesque destination located in the serene Olavan region of Radhanagari taluka, Kolhapur, Maharashtra. This stunning waterfall cascades down from a height of approximately 100 feet and is surrounded by lush greenery, offering breathtaking views.

Kaneri Math

Kaneri Math, also known as Shri Kshetra Siddhagiri Math, is a historic place and the highest seat of the Kadsiddheshwar tradition, located in Kaneri, Kolhapur district, Maharashtra, showcasing village life through sculptures and a Shiva temple.

Culture and Festivals

Kolhapur is known for its vibrant culture, traditional dance forms, and festivals. The city celebrates festivals like Ganesh Chaturthi, Gudi Padwa, and Diwali with great enthusiasm and devotion. The region is also famous for its unique Kolhapuri Chappals and handicrafts.
