Mahalaxmi Temple

The Mahalaxmi Temple is one of the most revered temples in Maharashtra, dedicated to the goddess Mahalaxmi. It is an important pilgrimage site and a historical landmark of Kolhapur.There are many Hindu temples located in the Maharashtra state but one of the most famous temples is “Kolhapur Mahalakshmi Temple”. This Temple also known with the name of Amba Bai Temple is located in Kolhapur, Maharashtra. So the temple is purely dedicated to the Lord Adi Parashakti Ji she is a form of Mahalakshmi Mata Ji.The Kolhapur Mahalaxmi Temple is of special religious significance being one of the six places where it is believed that one can either obtain salvation from desires or have them fulfilled. The temple takes its name from Mahalaxmi, the consort of Vishnu, and it is believed that the divine couple resides in the area.As per Hindu mythology, the Temple of Mahalaxmi Kolhapur is regarded as one among the 108 Shakti Peethas mentioned in several Hindu puranic texts. There many devotees have dreamed of attending the Holy Aarti of Mahalaxmi Temple but most of the time they miss Aarti. Because they don’t know the correct timings of Aarti.The architecture of Kolhapur Shaktipeeth Temple belongs to the Chalukya Empire and it is said that the temple was first built by Karnadeva around 700 AD. The Mahalakshmi idol of Kolhapur is made of sandstone and weighs 40 kilograms. The goddess wears a beautiful jeweled crown on her head. The height of Mahalaxmi goddess idol is about 2 feet 9 inches tall. Shree Mahalakshmi Devi's vehicle Lion, made of stone stands behind the Idol of the goddess. Shree Mahalakshmi Devi has four hands holding Mahalunga fruit (lemon fruit), Kaumodaki (mace), a shield, and a Panpatra. In Hinduism, almost all sacred idols are seen facing east or north. But Shree Mahalakshmi faces west. It is the architect's excellence, on 6 days from January 31st to February 2nd and November 9th to 11th, the rays of the sun coming from the Mahadwar fall directly on the face of the goddess Mahalakshmi.
